# The StaXX guide

StaXX is the page where every container on your server lives, drawn as a form so you can change a
setting without editing a file. This guide explains what you are looking at once it is installed and
running — what the marks on a row mean, and what StaXX does on your behalf and why.

This guide is written in English only.

## Pages that exist

- **[Checking for updates](updates.md)** — answers what the update column is telling you, why some
  images get checked more often than others, and what happens when you press it.
- **[What the marks mean](marks.md)** — a quick key to every mark on the stack list: running state,
  restart pending, pinned, and the rest.
- **[Where StaXX keeps its things](where-things-live.md)** — what lives in the data store versus the
  three lines on the flash drive, and the three ways to change where StaXX appears if you cannot
  reach its page.
- **[Sharing a stack with somebody else](sharing-a-stack.md)** — how Export makes a copy with your
  passwords, keys and paths taken out, what it refuses to send at all, and what the person on the
  other end has to fill in before it will run.
